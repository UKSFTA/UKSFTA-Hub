### **SESSION 1: NAVIGATION & PATROL MANEUVERS (2 HOURS)**

**MODULE A: THE NAVIGATION PHASE (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Navigate a 5km cross-country route through varying terrain using map and compass only.
    
- **Parameters:** Candidate must reach 4 designated waypoints in sequence. Night operations may be required.
    
- **Evaluation:** Pace counting, terrain association, and timing.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Missing a waypoint or exceeding the 60-minute time limit.
    

**MODULE B: PATROL FORMATIONS & COMMUNICATION (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Lead a 4-man patrol through high-threat areas using appropriate formations and hand signals.
    
- **Parameters:** Transition between staggered column, file, and diamond formations based on terrain and threat levels.
    
- **Evaluation:** Situational awareness, signal clarity, and formation integrity.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Total loss of formation control or "Blue-on-Blue" risk during simulated contact.


### **SESSION 2: TACTICAL CONTACT & MEDIC (2 HOURS)**

**MODULE C: CONTACT DRILLS (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Execute rapid break-contact and assault-through-contact drills during a simulated ambush.
    
- **Parameters:** Use of suppressive fire, smoke, and bounds to break contact or neutralize the threat.
    
- **Evaluation:** Reaction time, fire discipline, and communication under pressure.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Flagging a teammate or failing to achieve fire superiority.
    

**MODULE D: PATROL MEDIC (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Treat a casualty under fire and coordinate a CASEVAC.
    
- **Parameters:** Use of TCCC protocols (MARCH) while maintaining security.
    
- **Evaluation:** Accuracy of medical treatment and clarity of the 9-Line MEDEVAC request.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Death of the casualty due to improper tourniquet application or failure to call for CASEVAC.


### **SAFETY & CONDUCT**

1. **WEAPONS:** All platforms remain "Cold" until the Module C kinetic window.
    
2. **ACRE2:** Proper radio discipline is required at all times.
    
3. **EQUIPMENT:** Standard patrol order with full kit is mandatory.
