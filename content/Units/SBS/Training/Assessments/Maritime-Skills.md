### **SESSION 1: AMPHIBIOUS INFILTRATION (2 HOURS)**

**MODULE A: DIVING & SUB-SURFACE (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Infiltrate a coastline using SCUBA/Rebreather gear from a launch point 500m offshore.
    
- **Parameters:** Maintain depth control and buddy-pair integrity. Successful beach landing at the designated RV.
    
- **Evaluation:** Air management, navigation under water, and low-profile surfacing.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Surfacing early in sight of the objective or loss of buddy contact.
    

**MODULE B: BOAT HANDLING & COXING (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Pilot a Rigid-Hulled Inflatable Boat (RHIB) through a simulated harbour entrance or coastal obstacle course.
    
- **Parameters:** High-speed maneuvers, tactical beaching, and recovery.
    
- **Evaluation:** Precision, speed control, and engine management.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Collision with obstacles or failure to secure the boat upon beaching.


### **SESSION 2: COASTAL RECCE & DEMOLITIONS (2 HOURS)**

**MODULE C: BEACH RECONNAISSANCE (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Conduct a beach survey and identify coastal defenses/obstacles.
    
- **Parameters:** Using a laser rangefinder and maritime chart, identify 3 specific PIRs.
    
- **Evaluation:** Data accuracy, camouflage in the littoral zone, and report clarity.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Discovery by coastal patrol or incorrect beach gradient reporting.
    

**MODULE D: MARITIME DEMOLITIONS (60 MINS)**

- **Objective:** Set a simulated charge on a sub-surface target (e.g., harbor gate or ship hull).
    
- **Parameters:** Secure the charge while maintaining stability in a tidal current.
    
- **Evaluation:** Rigging accuracy and time-to-target.
    
- **Critical Fail:** Detonator safety breach or charge displacement.


### **SAFETY & CONDUCT**

1. **DIVING:** All divers must remain within 10m of their buddy at all times.
    
2. **SIGNALING:** Maritime flares are for emergency use only.
    
3. **EQUIPMENT:** Drysuits and tactical vests are required.
